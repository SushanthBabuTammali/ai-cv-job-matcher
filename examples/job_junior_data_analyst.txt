Junior Data Analyst — Example Retail Group (fictional advert for demo purposes)
Location: Cardiff (hybrid)

The role
You will turn sales and customer data into clear reports and dashboards that help our
commercial team make better decisions.

Responsibilities
- Write SQL queries to extract and clean data from our data warehouse
- Build and maintain Power BI dashboards and weekly KPI reporting
- Carry out exploratory data analysis to spot trends in sales and customer behaviour
- Support customer segmentation and forecasting projects
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders

Essential
- Degree in a numerate subject such as Data Science, Mathematics or Computer Science
- Strong SQL and Excel skills
- Experience with Python (pandas) for data cleaning and analysis
- Good communication and problem-solving skills

Desirable
- Experience with Tableau or Looker
- Knowledge of statistics and A/B testing
- Exposure to cloud platforms such as Azure or AWS
- Familiarity with Git
